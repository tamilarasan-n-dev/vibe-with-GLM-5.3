# Design Taste

- Prefers a dark, premium, minimal design language (matte black / deep graphite backgrounds, soft white text, electric blue / cyan / violet accents) inspired by Apple, Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Raycast, and Warp Terminal. Confidence: 0.9
- Avoids generic template looks, card spam, childish icons, Bootstrap-style UI, and large colorful gradients; every element should feel intentional, not decorative. Confidence: 0.9
- Uses glassmorphism, blur, glow, and noise textures sparingly rather than everywhere. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers cinematic motion design: 60 FPS, buttery smooth animations, easing on every animation, and no sudden appearance — everything should feel "expensive." Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers a premium typography pairing: Geist for headings, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for code. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers smooth scroll to feel responsive and direct (near 1:1 wheel response, minimal smoothing lag) — rejects heavy-dampened easing that makes the page feel slow or floaty. Confidence: 0.7
